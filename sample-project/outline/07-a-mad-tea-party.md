# 7 — A Mad Tea-party

**One purpose:** Deliver the set-piece of pure nonsense logic, and let Alice finally walk out on it.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Mad Hatter, the March Hare, the Dormouse

**Key events:**

- A crowded tea-table where there is somehow never room.
- 'Why is a raven like a writing-desk?' — a riddle with no answer.
- The Hatter's watch tells the day, not the hour; he has quarrelled with Time.
- The Dormouse's tale of three girls living in a treacle-well.
- Alice leaves in disgust and finds a door back into the hall.
