# 2 — Pool of Tears

**One purpose:** Strand Alice at the wrong size and give her the first talking-animal encounter she mishandles.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Mouse, the White Rabbit; referenced: Dinah

**Key events:**

- The cake makes Alice grow to nine feet tall.
- She cries a great pool of tears.
- The White Rabbit drops his gloves and fan; the fan shrinks her again.
- She swims in the pool of her own tears and meets the Mouse.
- She offends the Mouse by chattering about Dinah the cat.
