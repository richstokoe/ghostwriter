# 5 — Advice from a Caterpillar

**One purpose:** Give Alice a maddening interrogation about identity, and the tool (the mushroom) she will use for the rest of the book.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Caterpillar; referenced: the Pigeon

**Key events:**

- A hookah-smoking Caterpillar demands, 'Who are you?'
- Alice cannot say, having changed sizes so often.
- She recites 'You Are Old, Father William' and gets it all wrong.
- The Caterpillar tells her the mushroom changes her size.
- Her neck shoots up like a serpent's and a Pigeon accuses her of being a snake after its eggs.
