# 4 — The Rabbit sends in a Little Bill

**One purpose:** Trap Alice in the Rabbit's house at the wrong scale and play the comedy of scale for real stakes.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the White Rabbit, Bill the Lizard; referenced: Pat, Mary Ann

**Key events:**

- The Rabbit mistakes Alice for his maid Mary Ann and sends her for gloves.
- She drinks from a bottle and grows until she fills the house.
- The Rabbit and Pat try to get her out.
- Bill the Lizard is sent down the chimney and kicked back out.
- Pebbles thrown at her turn to little cakes; eating one shrinks her, and she escapes.
