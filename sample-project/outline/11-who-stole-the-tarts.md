# 11 — Who Stole the Tarts?

**One purpose:** Assemble the whole cast for the trial and start Alice growing again, toward her exit.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the King of Hearts, the Queen of Hearts, the Knave of Hearts, the White Rabbit, the Mad Hatter, the Cook, the Dormouse

**Key events:**

- The court is assembled; the Knave is charged with stealing the Queen's tarts.
- The King, as judge, muddles the procedure.
- The Hatter gives nervous, useless testimony.
- The Cook refuses to testify; pepper is everywhere.
- Alice, called as a witness, notices she is growing again.
