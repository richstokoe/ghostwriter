# 6 — Pig and Pepper

**One purpose:** Push Alice through the Duchess's chaos to the Cheshire Cat, who frames the madness of everyone ahead.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Duchess, the Cook, the Cheshire Cat; referenced: the baby, the Footmen

**Key events:**

- A Fish-Footman and a Frog-Footman trade an invitation on the doorstep.
- The pepper-filled kitchen; the Cook throwing things; the Duchess nursing a howling baby.
- Alice carries the baby outside and it turns into a pig.
- The Cheshire Cat explains that everyone here is mad and points her to the Hatter and the March Hare.
- The Cat vanishes slowly, leaving only its grin.
