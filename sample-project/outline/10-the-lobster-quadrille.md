# 10 — The Lobster Quadrille

**One purpose:** Let the nonsense turn to song and dance before tipping toward the trial.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Gryphon, the Mock Turtle

**Key events:**

- The Mock Turtle and Gryphon describe and perform the Lobster Quadrille.
- The whiting, the porpoise, and why lobsters matter to the dance.
- Alice recites ''Tis the Voice of the Lobster' and it comes out wrong.
- The Mock Turtle sings 'Beautiful Soup'.
- A cry of 'The trial's beginning!' pulls them away.
