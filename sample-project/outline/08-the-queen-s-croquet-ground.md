# 8 — The Queen's Croquet-Ground

**One purpose:** Introduce the Queen and the book's engine of arbitrary violence: 'Off with their heads!'

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Queen of Hearts, the King of Hearts, the White Rabbit, the Cheshire Cat; referenced: the gardeners

**Key events:**

- Card-gardeners frantically paint white roses red before the Queen sees.
- The royal procession arrives; the Queen orders beheadings on a whim.
- A croquet game played with flamingo mallets and hedgehog balls, all rules ignored.
- The Cheshire Cat's floating head starts an argument about whether a grin can be beheaded.
