# 12 — Alice's Evidence

**One purpose:** Break the trial and the dream, and return Alice to the ordinary afternoon she left.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the King, the Queen, the White Rabbit; referenced: her sister

**Key events:**

- Alice, now huge, upsets the jury-box.
- The King invents 'Rule Forty-two' to remove her.
- The White Rabbit reads out a nonsense poem as evidence.
- 'Sentence first — verdict afterwards,' says the Queen.
- Alice declares them 'nothing but a pack of cards' and wakes on the bank beside her sister.
