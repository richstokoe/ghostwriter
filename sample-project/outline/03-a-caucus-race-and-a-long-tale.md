# 3 — A Caucus-race and a Long Tale

**One purpose:** Widen Wonderland to a whole cast, and satirise how they organise themselves.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Mouse, the Dodo, the Lory, the Duck, the Eaglet

**Key events:**

- A soaked crowd of birds and animals gathers on the bank.
- The Mouse tries to dry them with a dry history lesson.
- The Dodo proposes a Caucus-Race with no rules and no clear finish.
- Everyone wins; Alice must supply the prizes.
- The Mouse tells its long, sad tale; Alice offends the party again.
