# 1 — Down the Rabbit-Hole

**One purpose:** Fall Alice out of an ordinary afternoon and into Wonderland, and establish her curious, self-arguing voice.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the White Rabbit; referenced: her sister, Dinah

**Key events:**

- Alice, bored on the riverbank, sees a White Rabbit with a waistcoat and a watch.
- She follows it down the rabbit-hole and falls, slowly, for a very long time.
- She lands in a long hall of locked doors.
- A tiny golden key opens a tiny door onto a beautiful garden she cannot fit through.
- A bottle marked DRINK ME shrinks her; a cake marked EAT ME is her next hope.
