# 9 — The Mock Turtle's Story

**One purpose:** Slow into melancholy comedy and hand Alice off toward the Mock Turtle's backstory.

**Characters:** Alice (POV), the Duchess, the Queen, the Gryphon, the Mock Turtle

**Key events:**

- The Duchess attaches a heavy moral to everything Alice says.
- The Queen sends the Gryphon to take Alice to the Mock Turtle.
- The Mock Turtle weeps through his history.
- 'We called him Tortoise because he taught us.'
- The absurd school curriculum: Reeling, Writhing, and the branches of Arithmetic.
